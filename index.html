2. Western Monumental Waterfront (Belém)

A capital-intensive institutional district anchored by CCB, MAC/CCB, MAAT, and Karnart. Specializes in large-scale visual arts and operatic productions requiring expansive floorplates. Relies on suburban rail and tram networks, decoupling daytime museum hours (10:00–19:00) from evening operatic performances.

3. Northern Residential-Cultural Belt (Saldanha / Alvalade / Campo Grande)

Lisbon's mid-century expansion serving domestic, academic, and cinephile audiences. Houses major foundation campuses (CAM Gulbenkian), municipal estates (Palácio Pimenta, Museu Bordalo Pinheiro), arthouse cinemas (Cinema Medeia Nimas), stand-up rooms (Teatro Villaret), and local concert halls (Teatro Maria Matos).

4. Eastern Creative & Arena Corridor (Anjos / Marvila / Parque das Nações)

Exhibits a dual character: independent post-industrial warehouses and associations in Anjos and Marvila (BOTA, Teatro Meridional, Fábrica Braço de Prata) foster grassroots experimentation, while Parque das Nações (MEO Arena, Casino Lisboa) accommodates commercial touring spectacles.
